About Porto à Tua Maneira

Porto à Tua Maneira is a platform for personalized tourist experiences in Porto. We help every visitor discover activities, local restaurants and lesser-known spots.

Our goal is to provide a more authentic, simple and responsible way to get to know the city, bringing tourists closer to local businesses and culture.

Through our application you can create your profile, indicate your preferences and receive recommendations tailored to your travel style.

First-year targets: 100 bookings, 20 local partnerships, 50 exclusive contents sold, 500 app downloads and 2,000 social followers.
