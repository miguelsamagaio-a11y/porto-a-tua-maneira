Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about Porto à Tua Maneira, personalized itineraries, bookings, partners and privacy.

About Porto à Tua Maneira

What is Porto à Tua Maneira?

It is a prototype platform (web/mobile) for creating personalized tourist experiences in Porto. It helps visitors discover activities, restaurants and hidden corners.

How is it different from Google Maps or TripAdvisor?

Those platforms provide search and reviews; Porto à Tua Maneira focuses on creating itineraries personalized to the user's interests, budget and time.

Is the app free?

Downloads and account creation are free. Some premium features or exclusive content ("secret spots") may be paid.

Which languages are supported?

The prototype supports Portuguese and English. More languages may be added in the future.

Account & profile

Do I need an account?

Some features like saving favorites, purchases or making reservations require an account. Other recommendations can be seen without signing in.

How are itineraries personalized?

By creating a profile you indicate interests, budget, pace and time available; the platform returns suggested routes and activities tailored to those choices.

Can I edit suggested itineraries?

Yes — recommendations are a starting point and can be adjusted, reordered or replaced.

Secret spots & bookings

What are secret spots?

Paid exclusive content detailing hidden places, local tips and curated mini-guides for users seeking a more authentic experience.

How do I access secret spots after purchase?

The content becomes available in your account after purchase and can be accessed from the app or website.

How do reservations work?

Select the experience, choose date, time and number of participants and confirm through the checkout flow. Partners confirm bookings in their dashboard.

Partners & promotions

How can I become a partner?

Businesses can contact the partnerships team via the contact page and follow the onboarding steps. Requirements include valid licensing and quality standards.

Privacy & support

Does the app use my location?

Only with your consent. Location helps tailor nearby suggestions and navigation features.

Can I request data deletion?

Yes. You may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your personal data by contacting privacy@portoatuamaneira.pt.
